AI Research Paper Assistant

Upload one paper: answers questions with page-cited RAG, then discovers, ranks and explains similar papers from Semantic Scholar and arXiv.

  • LangGraph
  • LangChain agents
  • RAG
  • Exactly two LangChain agents orchestrated by a LangGraph StateGraph with conditional routing and parallel fan-out.
  • Similarity scores computed in Python (embeddings + cosine similarity); the LLM only explains them.
  • Final project of SDAIA Academy's Advanced Agentic AI Systems Engineering course, built with Hasan Alhussain.
Code Open in Colab

Air Passengers Forecasting

Time-series capstone at SDAIA Academy: 12-month forecasts of the classic monthly AirPassengers series with decomposition, benchmarks and leakage-safe validation.

  • AutoGluon
  • AutoARIMA
  • Time Series
  • STL decomposition, naive and seasonal-naive benchmarks, and an AutoGluon model zoo (Naive, SeasonalNaive, AutoETS, AutoARIMA, Theta).
  • Eight rolling origins: AutoARIMA reached a MASE of 0.647 vs 1.313 for the seasonal-naive benchmark.
Code
